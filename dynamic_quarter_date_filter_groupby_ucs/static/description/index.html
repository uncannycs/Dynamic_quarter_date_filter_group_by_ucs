Company-Specific Fiscal Year Configuration

Reads your company's fiscal year settings (fiscalyear_last_month) directly from configuration to automatically determine the 4 fiscal quarter boundaries.

Dynamic Search Bar Quarter Filters

Accurate "Group By: Quarter" in Views

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about dynamic fiscal quarter filtering and grouping.

1. What problem does this module solve?

By default, the system groups and filters dates using standard calendar quarters (January to March for Q1, April to June for Q2, July to September for Q3, and October to December for Q4). However, many businesses follow a financial year that starts in a different month (such as April, July, October, or February). This module automatically aligns all quarterly date filters and grouping options with your business's actual financial calendar rather than forcing calendar-year quarters.

2. How are the four quarters calculated if our financial year starts in April?

When your financial year begins in April, the four quarters are automatically configured as:
  • Q1: April, May, June
  • Q2: July, August, September
  • Q3: October, November, December
  • Q4: January, February, March
If your financial year begins in any other month (such as July or October), the system shifts all four quarters accordingly in consecutive 3-month blocks.

3. How does this module affect the search bar date filters?

When you click on date filters in the search bar and select Q1, Q2, Q3, or Q4, the system automatically applies the exact date range corresponding to your financial quarters instead of calendar quarters.

4. How does grouping by quarter work in lists, tables, and charts?

When you apply Group By > Quarter on any list, pivot table, or graph view, records are organized according to your financial quarters. This ensures your quarterly sales summaries, revenue reports, and purchasing metrics match your accounting calendar.

5. How are quarters displayed when a financial year spans two calendar years?

For financial years that run across two calendar years (for example, April 2024 to March 2025), the group headings clearly display the dual-year notation (such as Q1 2024-25 or Q4 2024-25). This prevents confusion between the calendar year and the financial year.

6. Which business areas and records are supported?

The dynamic quarterly filtering and grouping works across all business records that include a date or date-and-time field, including Quotations, Sales Orders, Customer Invoices, Vendor Bills, Purchase Orders, CRM Leads, Inventory Deliveries, Project Tasks, and Timesheets.

7. Does this module work for multi-company setups with different financial years?

Yes. In a multi-company environment where different business entities operate on different financial calendars (for example, one company starting in April and another starting in January), the quarterly filters and group headings automatically adapt to the active company you are currently viewing.

8. Does this support both simple date fields and date-time fields?

Yes. It works seamlessly on both standard date fields (such as Invoice Date or Order Date) and exact date-and-time fields (such as Creation Date or Confirmation Timestamp), taking into account proper timezone boundaries.

9. Can I still combine Quarter filters with Year and Month filters?

Yes. You can combine financial quarter filters with specific years or individual months just like standard filters, allowing you to narrow down your analysis to specific periods without any conflict.

10. Can I compare data between different quarters?

Yes. When using period comparison features in search views and reporting, the comparison periods will evaluate against your configured financial quarters, giving you accurate quarter-over-quarter financial growth and performance comparisons.

11. Does clicking or drilling down into a grouped quarter show the correct records?

Yes. When you expand a quarter group in a list view or drill down into a pivot table cell, the system opens only the records that fall within the precise starting and ending dates of that specific financial quarter.

12. Will users need training to use this module?

No. There is zero learning curve. The module enhances the existing search bar, filter dropdowns, and group-by menus that users already use every day. Users continue using the standard interface, but with accurate financial dates.

13. Do I need to create custom filters or reports manually?

No. You do not need to build custom date ranges or manual search filters for every quarter. The standard quarter options automatically update system-wide once your financial year starting month is set.

14. What happens if our organization changes its financial year schedule?

If your organization updates its financial year start month in settings, the system immediately recalculates the quarter boundaries, and all subsequent searches, filters, and quarterly groupings automatically reflect the new calendar.

15. Does this module modify or alter existing historical transaction data?

No. The module only adjusts how dates are filtered and grouped for viewing and reporting. It never modifies, overwrites, or alters your underlying transaction dates or record values.
